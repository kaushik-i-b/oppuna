Features

A calm space that stays on your phone

Journal, track moods, breathe, and talk with an on-device companion — all without an internet connection.

  • Daily plan

    A gentle, personalized wellness plan shaped around your mood, goals, and time.

  • Mood & journal

    Log feelings, write freely, and review patterns — searchable, editable, deletable.

  • Breathe & ground

    Guided breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, and a soft sleep wind-down.

  • On-device AI

    Chat with a local companion. Safety checks and guided fallbacks — no cloud.

  • Your control

    App lock, export everything as JSON, or permanently delete all data.

Privacy

Nothing you write, record, or track leaves your device. Oppuna blocks outbound network access in production — there is no path to the cloud.

No account

Open the app and begin. No sign-up, no email, no profile in the cloud.

No cloud

No tracking

No ads, analytics, or telemetry. Your mind is not a product.
