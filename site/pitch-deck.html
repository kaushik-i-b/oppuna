Problem

Therapy alone cannot cover the week

Most people who seek care get one hour with a professional—and then face the remaining 167 hours alone.

1
Hour in session
Guided, structured, scarce
167
Hours between sessions
Where anxiety spikes, habits form, and progress is won or lost
  • ~197M people in India live with mental disorders—treatment gaps remain large
  • Therapist supply cannot scale to continuous support; waitlists and cost limit frequency
  • Between sessions, people need somewhere private to process, track, and cope—today that gap is filled by silence, social media, or cloud apps that harvest intimate data
The gap is the rest of the week—not a shortage of meditation apps.

Sources: The Lancet Psychiatry · NIMHANS NMHS

Why now

Four forces make this investable now

AI maturity

Small on-device models finally run on phones—private AI no longer requires shipping journals to the cloud.

Demand surge

Post-pandemic awareness normalised seeking help; digital mental-health usage is mainstream, not niche.

Therapist shortage

Clinical capacity is constrained by waitlists and cost. Software that helps people between visits can extend scarce professional time.

Paid category exists

Consumers already subscribe to Calm, Headspace, Wysa and peers—digital mental wellness is a paid market.

Timing is right: capable on-device AI + proven willingness to pay in adjacent products.

Solution

What Oppuna actually does

An AI-powered emotional wellness platform—not a chatbot, and not a replacement for clinicians.

Before care

  • Name what’s happening
  • Journal privately
  • Arrive prepared for the first session

Day to day

  • On-device AI reflection
  • Mood tracking and coping tools
  • Breathing, grounding, sleep wind-down

Alongside care

  • Keep practising what helped
  • Export or delete data anytime
  • Future: share insights with a therapist—only with consent
Oppuna complements professional care. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace clinicians.
Private tools for reflection, mood, coping, and preparation—kept on the user’s device.

User value

Why someone would choose Oppuna

Hypothesis: users will pay if Oppuna consistently delivers private value they cannot get from a weekly session alone.

Immediate support

Guidance available when distress spikes—not only at the next appointment.

Private reflection

A space to process thoughts without creating a cloud account or sharing journals.

Therapy preparation

Designed to help users stay engaged and arrive better prepared for sessions.

Progress tracking

Mood and journal history that makes patterns easier to notice over time.

Always-on guidance

Coping tools and reflection available on demand, on the user’s phone.

Value hypothesis: private, always-available support that makes clinical time more useful—not a clinical outcome claim.

Appendix

Operations

Crisis routing & app size

Crisis routing

Local regex/pattern matching before generative replies. High-risk categories open a crisis screen with Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1-800-891-4416) and Emergency 112. Not clinical triage.

App size

~1.1 GB today mainly from bundled on-device Qwen GGUF via Play Asset Delivery. Plan: post-install model download, smaller quant, optional packs—target sub-200 MB core experience.
