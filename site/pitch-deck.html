Problem

People need support between sessions—without giving up privacy

Therapy helps, but most of life happens outside the consulting room. Digital tools often ask for trust users cannot verify.

  • Intimate thoughts are routed through accounts, cloud sync and third-party AI APIs
  • Users fear their most vulnerable moments being stored, analysed or leaked
  • In low-connectivity settings, many wellness apps simply stop working
  • Privacy policies are not enough—people want privacy by design
“I want help, but I don’t want anyone else to know what I’m going through.”

Founder story

Built from lived experience

I experienced anxiety personally, and CBT played an important role in my recovery. I realised that progress did not happen only during therapy sessions. It also came from recording my thoughts, recognising recurring patterns, practising healthier responses and making gradual lifestyle changes between sessions.

That experience inspired Oppuna—a private, non-judgmental space where people can journal, organise their thoughts and receive AI-assisted support before, between and after professional care.

“Therapy may happen periodically, but managing our thoughts happens every day.”

Personal founder experience—not a universal clinical claim.

Context

How CBT shaped the idea

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is a structured form of psychotherapy that helps people recognise connections between their thoughts, emotions and behaviours, and practise healthier responses.

Thoughts → Emotions → Behaviours → Reflection → Healthier responses

My experience with CBT showed me that progress did not happen only during therapy sessions. Recording thoughts, identifying recurring patterns, practising healthier responses and making gradual lifestyle changes between sessions also played an important role. This insight became the foundation for Oppuna.

Oppuna supports everyday reflection before, between and after professional care. It does not independently provide CBT, diagnose conditions or replace a qualified mental-health professional.

Solution

Oppuna supports the work that happens between sessions

Oppuna is an AI-assisted emotional-wellness companion designed for the time before, between and after professional care.

It gives people a private, non-judgmental space to express their thoughts, create journal entries, record important experiences and organise what they are feeling.

Proposed

Planned Premium Membership

Annual membership concept—subject to professional partnerships, pricing, availability, safeguarding and unit economics.

  • Full access to premium journaling and AI-assisted wellness features
  • Advanced mood, lifestyle and reflection insights
  • Personal progress summaries
  • Six 60-minute professional consultations included with the annual Premium membership
  • Consultations by appropriately qualified and independently verified mental-health professionals
  • User-controlled sharing of journals or summaries with the professional
  • Priority booking and professional-care referrals

Unit economics (required before final price)

Annual membership revenue
− cost of six consultations
− payment gateway and platform fees
− AI and infrastructure costs
− customer support costs
− taxes
= contribution margin per subscriber

No final subscription price until this contribution margin is sustainable.

If six included sessions are unsustainable

1. Six discounted consultation credits
2. Two included consultations plus four discounted sessions
3. Six shorter 20–30 minute wellness check-ins
4. Employer- or sponsor-funded consultations

Consultation availability, professional category and applicable terms may vary. Oppuna is not an emergency service. Consultations are not described as CBT unless delivered by a properly qualified professional who has specifically agreed to provide CBT.

Proposed only—until Oppuna has finalised professional partnerships, pricing, availability, safeguarding processes and unit economics.
