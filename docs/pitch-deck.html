Problem

People need support between sessions—without giving up privacy

Professional care can help, but most of life happens outside the consulting room. People still need a private space to process thoughts, record experiences and prepare for their next conversation.

  • Intimate thoughts are often routed through accounts, cloud storage and third-party AI services
  • Users may fear vulnerable information being stored, analysed or exposed
  • Privacy policies alone do not create trust—privacy must be built into the product
  • Professional support is essential but often periodic

Appendix

Context

Detailed CBT framework

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is a structured form of psychotherapy that helps people recognise connections between their thoughts, emotions and behaviours, and practise healthier responses.

Thoughts → Emotions → Behaviours → Reflection → Healthier responses

Between-session practice—thought records, pattern recognition and gradual lifestyle change—is a common component of many CBT programmes.

Oppuna supports everyday reflection around professional care. CBT-inspired reflection concepts may inform product design, but Oppuna does not provide CBT or deliver psychotherapy. Future CBT-inspired exercises will be designed and reviewed with qualified mental-health professionals.

Appendix

Engineering

App-size explanation and reduction plan

Current install size is approximately 1.1 GB primarily because the optional on-device Qwen GGUF (~941 MB) is delivered with the app via Play Asset Delivery.

Why it is large today

Bundled local model weights dominate package size. Core app code and assets are a small fraction of the total.

Technical reduction plan

· Model download after installation
· Smaller quantised model
· Optional model packs
· Remote fallback only with explicit consent
· Asset optimisation
· Android App Bundle delivery

A sub-200 MB Android download is a proposed target for the core experience without a bundled large model—not a guarantee while full local weights remain packaged.
