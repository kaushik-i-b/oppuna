The Problem

Mental wellness apps ask users to trade privacy for support.

🔓 Privacy erosion

Most wellness & AI apps require accounts, cloud sync, and analytics. Sensitive thoughts end up on someone else's server.

📡 Connectivity dependency

AI companions fail offline — on planes, in rural areas, during outages. Support shouldn't require a signal.

🤝 Trust deficit

Users are wary of "therapy AI" that phones home, trains on their data, or monetizes emotional content.

⚠️ Data risk

Breaches, subpoenas, and ad-targeting pipelines expose the most personal data users ever create.

"I want help, but I don't want my journal on someone's server."

The Solution

A complete wellness companion that never leaves your device.

🔒

No account

Open and use. No signup, no email, no identity trail.

☁️

No cloud

Everything stored locally in SQLite on your phone.

✈️

No internet

Works fully in airplane mode. Network guard blocks all outbound requests.

  • Gentle AI conversation (rule-based + optional on-device LLM)
  • Mood tracking, journaling, breathing, grounding, sleep support
  • Crisis safety routing with regional offline helplines
  • Export or permanently delete all your data anytime
