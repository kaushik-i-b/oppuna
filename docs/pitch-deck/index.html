The Problem

Mental wellness apps ask for your most private thoughts — then send them to the cloud.

Privacy erosion

Journal entries, mood logs, and AI chats stored on remote servers — subject to breaches and subpoenas.

Connectivity dependency

Most wellness apps fail offline or degrade without signal, excluding rural and travel contexts.

Trust deficit

Users with heightened privacy needs — journalists, activists, abuse survivors — are underserved.

Oversharing by design

Accounts, analytics, and ad SDKs are baked into incumbent business models.

Users want support — not surveillance.
