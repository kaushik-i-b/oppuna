# Voice-over

Read this after the picture is cut. Leave the conversation audio loud enough to hear Oppuna. The voice-over steps back whenever Oppuna or you are speaking in the scene.

Pace: calm, unhurried, no hype words. About 130 words a minute. This read is about 380 words, near 3 minutes with the pauses where the product speaks.

---

Most journaling apps begin with an empty text box.

When you’ve had a difficult day, explaining everything in writing can be the hardest part.

So we asked a different question.

What if reflection started with a conversation?

[Tap. Let the greeting play under silence.]

[After your first sentence, under Oppuna’s reply, only if there is room:]

You just talk. Oppuna listens in the same turn.

[After playback stops:]

AssemblyAI’s voice agent is the conversation. It transcribes as you speak, takes the turn, speaks back, and stops when you interrupt. There is no record button.

[Over the reflection card, after the choices are visible:]

Oppuna does not treat the conversation as permanent memory. It proposes a reflection, and you decide which lines survive.

[After the second reply:]

Oppuna isn’t remembering an unrestricted transcript. It’s continuing from context I explicitly allowed it to remember.

[Architecture slide:]

Your voice goes to AssemblyAI’s voice agent. That one session hears you, takes turns, speaks, and can stop when you interrupt. Oppuna’s tools turn the conversation into a reflection, a mood note, or a lookup of approved memory. What you check is what a later conversation can use. Everything else stays out.

[Close:]

We started with a simple idea.

Sometimes you don’t need another screen asking you to type how you feel.

Sometimes you just need somewhere to talk.

Oppuna turns those conversations into reflections, and lets you decide what deserves to be remembered.

Talk.

Reflect.

Remember — on your terms.

This is Oppuna Voice, powered by AssemblyAI.

These are not voice-over. Say them live, on the Listening label.

1. Today was exhausting. Something happened at work and I’ve been thinking about it all evening.
2. Wait — that’s not really what bothered me.
3. I think I’m more frustrated because I couldn’t switch off after I came home.
4. Can you turn this into today’s reflection? Leave out the argument.
5. I’ve been stressed again.

If take 5 does not connect to the approved lines, replace only that sentence with: I’ve been stressed again, especially after work.

## Fixed product line, not yours

The greeting is spoken by the voice agent from app text, not generated for the scene:

“Hi, I’m Oppuna. I’m a reflection companion, not a person and not a therapist. I’m listening.”

Let it play. Do not dub over it.
