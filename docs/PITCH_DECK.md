## Core (12 slides)

### 1. Vision
**Therapy lasts one hour. Recovery happens during the other 167.**

Oppuna is an AI-powered emotional wellness platform that supports people before, between, and after professional care.

*Takeaway: Answer “Why Oppuna?” in the first minute—continuity around care.*

### 2. Problem
Therapy alone cannot cover the week. People get ~1 hour with a clinician and face **167 hours** alone—where anxiety spikes, habits form, and progress is won or lost.

- ~197M people in India live with mental disorders; treatment gaps remain large  
- Therapist supply cannot scale to continuous support  
- The gap is filled by silence, social media, or cloud apps that harvest intimate data  

*Takeaway: The need is continuity of care—not another meditation library.*

### 3. Why Now
1. **AI maturity** — on-device models make private support viable  
2. **Demand surge** — digital mental health is mainstream  
3. **Therapist shortage** — software that extends care is infrastructure  
4. **Willingness to pay** — Calm, Headspace, Wysa prove a paid category  

*Takeaway: Category proven; trust and continuity still unsolved.*

### 4. Solution
Oppuna turns the other 167 hours into supported recovery—not a chatbot.

| Before care | Between sessions | After care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name what’s happening | 24/7 emotional support | Remember what helped |
| Capture concerns privately | Mood, journal, coping | Keep practising |
| Arrive prepared | Progress stays private unless shared | Future therapist collaboration (consent) |

Complements professional care. Does not diagnose, treat, or replace clinicians.

*Takeaway: Private continuity around care—on the user’s device, on their terms.*

### 5. Why Customers Will Pay
Users pay for outcomes weekly therapy cannot deliver alone:

- Immediate support (including 2am)  
- Less isolation  
- Better, higher-leverage sessions  
- Visible continuous progress  
- Affordable vs. an extra therapy session (Premium proposed ₹299/mo vs ₹1,000–₹3,000/session)  

*Takeaway: Customers pay for continuous emotional support that makes scarce clinical time work harder.*

### 6. Market Opportunity
| Layer | Planning range | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TAM** | ~$15–35B | Global digital mental health & wellness apps |
| **SAM** | ~$1–3B | EN/HI smartphone users seeking paid digital emotional support (India + similar markets) |
| **SOM (5yr)** | ~$30–80M | Privacy-conscious adults near professional care who pay for between-session continuity |

Beachhead: digitally active adults in India already in/near care.  
Assumption sketch: 10–25M addressable × 2–5% paid × ₹2–3K ARPU → SOM band.

*Takeaway: Capture the continuity layer of an existing paid category.*

### 7. Competition
Customers already pay:

| Company | Est. reach | Paid model | Core focus | Oppuna edge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wysa | 6M+ users | ~$75–$99/yr | AI CBT + coaching | On-device / privacy-first continuity |
| Woebot | Pioneer; consumer wound down 2025 | Enterprise | Structured CBT | Consumer continuity underserved |
| Headspace | 85M+ DL; ~2M paid | ~$70/yr | Meditation | Care continuity, not content |

*Takeaway: Not “will people pay?”—who earns their trust?*

Metrics: CAC by channel, activation, D7/D30, clinician referrals, paid conversion.

*Takeaway: Trust network first; paid growth once unit economics work.*
