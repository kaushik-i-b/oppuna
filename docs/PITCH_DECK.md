## Core (12 slides) — unique purpose each

### 1. Vision
State the positioning. Introduce Oppuna as AI-powered emotional wellness for the hours around professional care.

### 2. Problem *(only deep “167 hours” explanation)*
Therapy alone cannot cover the week. Stats + 1 vs 167 visual. Gap filled by silence, social media, or cloud apps.

### 3. Why now
AI maturity · demand surge · therapist shortage · paid category already exists (Calm/Headspace/Wysa).

### 4. Solution — what Oppuna does
Before care / day to day / alongside care. No problem restatement. Complements clinicians; does not replace them.

### 5. User value *(not pricing)*
Hypothesis: users pay if value is consistent. Immediate support · private reflection · therapy preparation · progress tracking · always-on guidance.  
No subscription/pricing copy here.

### 6. Market
TAM / SAM / SOM with labeled assumptions. Beachhead: adults in India near professional care.

### 7. Competition — differentiation
Wysa, Woebot, Headspace, Calm, Youper. Paid category exists; Oppuna differs on privacy architecture and clinician adjacency.

### 8. Business model *(not value)*
Who pays · what they pay · proposed pricing table · conversion funnel hypothesis · future B2B expansion.

### 9. GTM — three channels only
1. Psychologist referrals  
2. Organic content  
3. University & community pilots  

Employer/institutional → roadmap.

### 10. Traction — evidence only
100+ Play downloads (3 days) · 11 psychiatrist interviews · product milestones. Retention/revenue still unproven.
